Measure skin layers on H&E slides

Segments the stratum corneum, epidermis and glands, then measures layer thickness, area, EDJ tortuosity and section quality. Example results for two slides are shown alongside.

Choosing a folder needs Chrome or Edge.

  1. Choose a folder of images (.svs, .tif, .jpg, .png).
  2. Pick the scanner and press Run.
  3. Results are saved in a predicted_<folder> folder inside it; check and correct them on the Review page.

Runs on this computer in the browser (Chrome or Edge); images are not uploaded.
